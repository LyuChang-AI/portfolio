Research

Research on LLM behavior in digital gender-based violence scenarios, focusing on framing bias, victim-blaming, consent interpretation, and refusal thresholds. Methods include survey-based experiments, vignette design, qualitative discourse analysis, and comparative model evaluation, with an emphasis on AI safety, ethics, and gender justice.
